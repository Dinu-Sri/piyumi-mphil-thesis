# WUSL Thesis Formatting Instructions

Primary source: `source_documents/admin_guidelines_and_forms/New-WUSL-Thesis-PreprationGuidelines-Mphil_PhD.pdf`.

These notes are the local working rulebook for Word generation and final formatting. If a supervisor or the latest WUSL office instruction conflicts with this file, update this file and record the decision in `logs/decisions.md`.

## Paper, Font, And Spacing

- Paper size: A4, 210 mm x 297 mm.
- Font: Times New Roman throughout the thesis.
- Body text: 12 pt.
- Initial submission for evaluation: double line spacing.
- Final copy after evaluation: 1.5 line spacing.
- Print one side only for submitted copies.
- Use clear white good-quality A4 paper, at least 80 gsm, for final copies.

## Page Margins

- Left margin: 3.7 cm.
- Right margin while typing: 2.5 cm.
- Right margin after binding: expected to be 2.0 cm.
- Top margin: 3.5 cm.
- Bottom margin: 3.5 cm.
- Cover page margins: 3.5 cm on all sides.

## Required Order Of Front Matter

Use this order before the main body:

1. Title page.
2. Declaration page.
3. Supervisor's certificate.
4. Acknowledgements.
5. Abstract page.
6. Table of contents.
7. List of tables.
8. List of figures.
9. List of abbreviations.

## Page Numbering

- Title page is not numbered.
- Numbering begins with the Declaration page.
- Front matter uses lowercase Roman numerals at the bottom center.
- Main text begins with Arabic numerals from the Introduction.
- Page numbers are placed 10 mm above the midpoint of the bottom edge.
- Page number font: Times New Roman.

## Headings

Front matter titles, chapter numbers, and chapter titles:

- Uppercase.
- Times New Roman.
- 14 pt.
- Bold.
- Centered.

Chapter number:

- Example: `CHAPTER 1`.
- Uppercase, Times New Roman, 14 pt, bold, centered.

Chapter title:

- Example: `INTRODUCTION`.
- Uppercase, Times New Roman, 14 pt, bold, centered.

First-level heading:

- Sentence case.
- Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold.
- Left aligned.

Second-level heading:

- Sentence case.
- Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold.
- Left aligned.

Third-level heading:

- Sentence case.
- Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold.
- 10 mm tab position.
- Justified.

## Paragraphs

- Body text: Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- Use double spacing for initial submission and 1.5 spacing for final post-evaluation copy.
- Maintain consistent spacing between chapter number, chapter title, subtitles, and body paragraphs.
- Paragraphs may be separated by one-line spacing or by a 0.5 mm first-line tab/indent.

## Recommended Chapter Layout

This project currently follows the standard six-chapter thesis layout:

1. Chapter 1: Introduction.
2. Chapter 2: Literature Review.
3. Chapter 3: Materials and Methods / Methodology.
4. Chapter 4: Results or first research chapter: Development of Enhanced Coating for a Scintillation Detector.
5. Chapter 5: Discussion or second research chapter: Development of Personal Radiation Monitoring Network.
6. Chapter 6: Conclusions, Recommendations and Future Suggestions.
7. References.
8. Appendices.

Results and discussion may be combined in a chapter when appropriate.

## Abstract

- Maximum length: 600 words.
- Should outline justification, methodology, significant findings, and conclusions.
- Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- Double spacing according to the specimen.

## Figures

- Place figures close to the relevant text.
- Do not put borders around illustrations unless scientifically necessary.
- Use good-quality images; graphics should normally be at least 300 dpi.
- Figure title is placed below the figure.
- Figure title: sentence case, Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold, 1.5 line spacing.
- If a figure title has more than one line, justify it.
- Figure caption/explanation: sentence case, Times New Roman, 11 pt, 1.5 line spacing, justified.
- Include legends and statistical significance where applicable.
- Maps should include coordinates, a linear scale, direction arrow, and index map where relevant.

## Tables

- Place tables close to the relevant text.
- Tables should be self-explanatory.
- Do not repeat data already given elsewhere in the text.
- Use only relevant data; large data sets should go to appendices.
- Avoid vertical lines.
- Avoid shading in table cells unless there is a specific reason.
- Use major horizontal lines.
- Table title is placed above the table.
- Table title: sentence case, Times New Roman, 12 pt, bold, 1.5 line spacing.
- Text inside tables: sentence case, Times New Roman, 11 pt, 1.5 line spacing, left aligned.
- Numbers inside tables: Times New Roman, 11 pt, 1.5 line spacing, right aligned.
- Column headings: sentence case, Times New Roman, 11 pt, 1.5 line spacing, centered.
- Table footnotes: sentence case, Times New Roman, 10 pt, italic, 1.5 line spacing, justified.
- Cite the source in a table footnote if the table is not original.

## References

- WUSL guideline says Harvard referencing is preferred.
- In-text citations use author-year style.
- Single author: surname and year.
- Two authors: both surnames and year.
- Three or more authors: first surname followed by `et al.` and year.
- All references cited in text should appear in the References section.
- References should be arranged alphabetically at the end of the thesis.
- Multiple works by the same author in the same year should use `a`, `b`, etc.
- Unpublished results and personal communications may be mentioned in the text without adding them to the reference list.
- Reference page heading: `REFERENCES`, Times New Roman, 14 pt, centered, bold.
- Reference entries: Times New Roman, 12 pt, double spacing, justified.
- Do not add extra space between references unless the supervisor or format editor requests it.

## Abbreviations And Scientific Terms

- Define unconventional abbreviations at first use.
- Acceptable Latin terms include `e.g.`, `i.e.`, `et al.`, `in vitro`, and `in vivo`.
- Non-English terms except proper nouns should be italicized.
- Scientific names of organisms should be italicized. This is less central to the current detector thesis but remains a thesis-wide rule.

## Title And Cover

Use `source_documents/admin_guidelines_and_forms/title-page-of-theses-preparation-guide.pdf` for title page and cover page examples.

Cover-page style:

- Title: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 18 pt, gold-colored font.
- Student name: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 18 pt, gold-colored font.
- Degree: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 18 pt, gold-colored font.
- University and year: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 18 pt, gold-colored font.
- M.Phil. hard-bound cover color: green.

Title-page style:

- Title: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- Student name: uppercase, centered, bold, Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- Submission statement: sentence case, centered, Times New Roman, 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing.

## Word Builder Defaults For This Project

The default build tool should use:

- A4 page size.
- Margins: left 3.7 cm, right 2.5 cm, top 3.5 cm, bottom 3.5 cm.
- Times New Roman, 12 pt.
- 1.5 line spacing for final mode.
- Double spacing for initial-submission mode.
- Centered footer page number.
