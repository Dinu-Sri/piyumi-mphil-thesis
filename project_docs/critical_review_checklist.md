# Scientific Critical Review Checklist

Use this before calling a chapter supervisor-review ready.

## Thesis Logic

- The research problem is clear.
- The research gap is specific.
- Objectives match the methods.
- Methods can answer the research questions.
- Results are not overstated.
- Conclusions are limited to what the evidence supports.

## Materials Science Phase

- Coating material selection is justified.
- Binder and solvent choices are explained.
- CsI(Tl) hygroscopicity is handled consistently.
- The 550 nm emission peak is used correctly in optical evaluation.
- Absorbance, transmittance, diffuse reflectance, adhesion, thickness, and stability are not confused.
- TiO2 + PVP selection is supported by the reported evidence.

## PRD Network Phase

- The network phase is framed as application/system integration unless new data support a larger claim.
- SiPM, PCB, XBee3, mesh network, firmware processing, and CAS claims are technically consistent.
- Power, bandwidth, latency, reliability, and field-use claims are supported or qualified.

## Presentation Quality

- Figures are readable and cited in text.
- Tables have units and clear headings.
- Acronyms are introduced before use.
- No TODO comments remain in final output.
- Repeated claims are consolidated instead of scattered.
